# Security policy

## Account access

Connect through Hidden Jobs sign-in and review the application's access before
approving. Your MCP client manages connection credentials. Keep them out of
prompts, screenshots, logs and source control.

Use your client's disconnect and reconnect controls when needed. Removing a
local server entry does not guarantee that account authorization has been revoked.
Keep administrative credentials and private customer data out of this repository.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Contact the maintainers privately with a description, affected endpoint,
reproduction steps without credentials and the potential impact. Do not open a
public issue containing sensitive details. Allow time for assessment and
remediation before disclosure.
